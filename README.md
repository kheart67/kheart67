### ♡ Behind the art

Hi, I’m **Heart**, also known as **KHeart67**. I’m an artist and aspiring animator drawn to expressive characters, dreamy worlds, and stories told through color and movement.

My little corner brings together pastel goth aesthetics, art, music, and the freedom to be a little different. I’m learning, experimenting, and finding my voice one project at a time.
