The soul behind the screen

A creative heart.
A curious mind.

I’m Heart, an artist, aspiring animator, and music lover. I’m drawn to expressive worlds, thoughtful details, and visual storytelling. Portraits, illustrations, and animation give me different ways to explore the feelings and ideas I want to share.

I learn through images, examples, and hands-on experiments. This portfolio is a space for my finished work and the studies, discoveries, and small steps that help me grow.

What shapes the way I create

More than an aesthetic.

Reflections from my strengths mind map: creativity, care, curiosity, and self-awareness.

✧

Creative explorer

I’m imaginative, curious, and open to new ideas. My appreciation for art, music, and beauty gives me different starting points for visual expression.

In my creative workI explore references, experiment with styles, and turn ideas into portraits, illustrations, and animation studies.

♡

Compassionate collaborator

Fairness, kindness, and honesty guide how I work with people. I value teamwork and want others to feel heard and respected.

When working togetherI aim to listen carefully, respect different perspectives, and communicate thoughtfully about shared ideas.

☾

Thoughtful visual learner

I examine different sides of an idea and understand best by seeing and doing. Examples, images, and practical exploration help me learn.

In my processI use sketches, visual plans, references, and manageable learning steps to develop my understanding.

✿

Self-aware striver

I recognize my fears and areas for growth while continuing to move forward. Reflection helps me notice what I need and where I can improve.

As I growI’m learning to ask for feedback, manage stress, and treat setbacks as information I can use.

Still figuring things out. Still moving forward.

I’m building confidence in speaking up, developing consistent creative habits, and using small deadlines to finish projects. My goal is to support the creativity, care, and curiosity I already have with structure and steady action.
